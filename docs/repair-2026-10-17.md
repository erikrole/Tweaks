# Tweaks 0.1.1 repair verification — 2026-10-17

This follows the [original audit](verification-2026-10-17.md). The audit remains a historical record of the failures in 0.1.0. Version 0.1.1 (build 3) repairs all ten confirmed defects and adds 26 permanent regression cases, including a further layout issue caught during installed verification.

## Repairs

1. Settings rows join their parent before parent-relative constraints activate. Construction now succeeds with share, application and audio rows. Installed verification then exposed transient zero-size stack constraint conflicts: document columns now use Auto Layout with a usable initial width and are measured before display. A regression checks usable geometry before display and on all five tabs afterward.
2. `autoConnectShareURLs` stores configured servers separately from the Boolean switch. Legacy arrays and disabled switches migrate without overwriting either intent; editing the list does not enable the tweak.
3. Caps Lock uses public native HID service APIs. Original delays are recorded per keyboard and boot, restored individually, and consumed only after successful restoration. Re-enabling captures a new original. Table-format parsing covers historical `hidutil` evidence without flattening mixed device values.
4. Shortcut collisions compare physical key codes and modifiers. Explicit saved assignments take precedence over defaults. Carbon handler/hotkey registration failures are reported in the settings control, including macOS's error code.
5. Downloaded-image detection respects standardized directory boundaries and resolves existing ancestors, rejecting sibling directories, traversal and escaping symlinks.
6. DMG updates retain a backup through atomic replacement, then move the old app to Trash. Failed Trash keeps the backup and reports its path. Failed eject retains the downloaded image and reports the incomplete cleanup.
7. Audio discovery, listeners and caps cover master or individual writable output channels. Capping never raises a quieter channel. Suppression compares all channel values, so a hardware-rounded first channel cannot hide another channel's increase.
8. Sleep-remount obligations persist outside retry tasks and across app restarts. They survive repeated sleep, cancelled/in-flight mounts, and unmounts that finish after wake. Retries continue after the initial twelve attempts, with network-change triggers and one-minute backoff.
9. Option-green explicitly toggles the window's supported `AXFullScreen` property. Both full-screen and zoom green-button subroles are recognized. Unsupported AX windows retain native handling.
10. A running Archive Utility causes ZIP processing to defer through the watcher's existing retry mechanism instead of forgetting the arrival.

The Keep Awake panel now describes observable activity instead of claiming exact upload/download/NAS-copy completion. It shows the thresholds and paused/slow-job limits. Clipboard cleanup and sleep-eject descriptions also state their timing and busy-drive limitations.

## Installed checks

- All five settings tabs open with readable controls, including configured shares
  and application rows. Final Debug/Release logs have no constraint or negative
  geometry faults. Installed runtime logs have no remaining settings constraint
  conflicts. The remaining AppKit negative-size warnings were symbolized to
  `NSThemeFrame._positionSharingIndicator` and
  `NSWindowSharingSessionRecipientIndicator` during window-sharing notifications;
  they are not evidence of a Tweaks settings-layout failure.
- Toggling Caps Lock delay off restored the keyboard's HID value to
  **250,000,000 ns**; turning it back on set **0**. The original enabled choice
  was restored. This verifies the live HID setting, not physical keystroke timing.
- Existing Accessibility permission was refreshed with the user's local
  authentication. The stale record referenced an Apple Development identity;
  the refreshed allowed record matches the installed Developer ID team. The app
  reports trusted access and the permission banner is gone.
- Every pre-existing preference from the pre-replacement snapshot is unchanged,
  including switches, configured shares and saved shortcuts. Added keys contain
  the per-keyboard restoration state and macOS file-dialog bookkeeping. New
  optional features remain off. No Tweaks-owned sleep assertion remains after the
  temporary Keep Awake check was stopped.
- The intermediate installed build 2 successfully exported a fixture through the
  host's Quick Image Actions panel: a 1,024 × 1,024 JPEG copy, with the original PNG
  hash unchanged. The image-export implementation is unchanged in build 3.
- The final installed Finder extension is running. Finder's transient Action and
  context menus could not be inspected through the UI automation surface: it
  returned the Desktop while a menu was open, and Escape restored the same Finder

  therefore **not visually verified**, and no Finder crash is claimed.

## Remaining physical acceptance

This repair pass does not claim physical media-key delivery, actual notification-stack dismissal, full-screen/window-layout interaction through real input, a full sleep/wake or login cycle, NAS/VPN restoration, external-drive eject, USB/Bluetooth changes or monitor reconnect. Those need their real devices and workloads. Slow/paused job completion remains explicitly heuristic. No user app was replaced by the DMG fixture tests and no real disk was ejected.

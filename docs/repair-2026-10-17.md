# Tweaks 0.1.1 repair verification — 2026-10-17

This follows the [original audit](verification-2026-10-17.md). The audit remains a historical record of the failures in 0.1.0. Version 0.1.1 (build 2) repairs all ten confirmed defects and adds 25 permanent regression cases.

## Repairs

1. Settings rows join their parent before parent-relative constraints activate. Construction now succeeds with share, application and audio rows.
2. `autoConnectShareURLs` stores configured servers separately from the Boolean switch. Legacy arrays and disabled switches migrate without overwriting either intent; editing the list does not enable the tweak.
3. Caps Lock uses public native HID service APIs. Original delays are recorded per keyboard and boot, restored individually, and consumed only after successful restoration. Re-enabling captures a new original. Table-format parsing covers historical `hidutil` evidence without flattening mixed device values.
4. Shortcut collisions compare physical key codes and modifiers. Explicit saved assignments take precedence over defaults. Carbon handler/hotkey registration failures are reported in the settings control, including macOS's error code.
5. Downloaded-image detection respects standardized directory boundaries and resolves existing ancestors, rejecting sibling directories, traversal and escaping symlinks.
6. DMG updates retain a backup through atomic replacement, then move the old app to Trash. Failed Trash keeps the backup and reports its path. Failed eject retains the downloaded image and reports the incomplete cleanup.
7. Audio discovery, listeners and caps cover master or individual writable output channels. Capping never raises a quieter channel. Suppression compares all channel values, so a hardware-rounded first channel cannot hide another channel's increase.
8. Sleep-remount obligations persist outside retry tasks and across app restarts. They survive repeated sleep, cancelled/in-flight mounts, and unmounts that finish after wake. Retries continue after the initial twelve attempts, with network-change triggers and one-minute backoff.
9. Option-green explicitly toggles the window's supported `AXFullScreen` property. Both full-screen and zoom green-button subroles are recognized. Unsupported AX windows retain native handling.
10. A running Archive Utility causes ZIP processing to defer through the watcher's existing retry mechanism instead of forgetting the arrival.

The Keep Awake panel now describes observable activity instead of claiming exact upload/download/NAS-copy completion. It shows the thresholds and paused/slow-job limits. Clipboard cleanup and sleep-eject descriptions also state their timing and busy-drive limitations.

## Remaining physical acceptance

This repair pass does not claim physical media-key delivery, actual notification-stack dismissal, a full sleep/wake cycle, NAS/VPN restoration, external-drive eject, USB/Bluetooth changes or monitor reconnect. Those need their real devices and workloads. Slow/paused job completion remains explicitly heuristic. No user app was replaced by the DMG fixture tests and no real disk was ejected.
